    Fig.04 / The Process

    Four steps. Scoped per project.
    One unified core.

    Engagement-light, hands-on with the operators. We don't run agency theatre — we sit beside your team, audit what's already running, then ship the connective tissue your stack should have had on day one.

    4 PHASES · SCOPED PER PROJECT 2 OPERATORS · 1 PM

    Fig.04 — Process Map PHASE 01 / 04
    01 PHASE 01 · KICK-OFF

    Discover. Map every wire.

    Whatever shape the work takes, we start the same way. We sit with the operators, watch the actual workflow, and inventory every tool that's quietly load-bearing. Nothing assumed.

    Out the other side: a diagram of the current world you can point at, the friction points named, and a one-pager of what "good" looks like that the team can sign off.

    Deliverables 04
    • →Stack diagram of the current world
    • →Named friction points · ranked
    • →Success criteria · one-pager, signed
    • →Scope agreement & operator interviews
    Fig.04 — Process Map PHASE 02 / 04
    02 PHASE 02 · DEEP DIVE

    Audit. Quantify the leak.

    We chase the leak. Where are people manually re-typing? Where's reconciliation broken? Which hand-offs fail in silence? We name the cost in euros and hours, not adjectives.

    The output is a prioritized rebuild list, an honest ROI projection, and a clear call on the one or two surfaces that have to ship first.

    Deliverables 04
    • →Audit report · 14–22 pages
    • →Prioritized rebuild list (P0–P3)
    • →ROI projection · annualized
    • →Ship-first call · 1–2 surfaces
    Fig.04 — Process Map PHASE 03 / 04
    03 PHASE 03 · THE BUILD

    Integrate. Wire the core.

    The longest phase. We wire the connective tissue — APIs, webhooks, schemas, queues — and build the normalized data layer underneath (Postgres on Supabase is our usual pick; not the only one). The system starts breathing.

    Schemas get documented as we go. Operators see the shape of the data weekly, on staging. No surprises at the end.

    Deliverables 04
    • →Live integrations & webhooks
    • →Normalized data layer · documented
    • →Operational surfaces · production-ready
    • →Staging environment · weekly demos
    Fig.04 — Process Map PHASE 04 / 04
    04 PHASE 04 · ONGOING

    Operate. Stay close.

    The system ships into operators' hands. They get trained — properly, in their own language, with runbooks they actually open. Alerting goes live. We sit in your Slack.

    Then we stay on retainer to iterate. Every operational layer we've shipped is still running on the same core, long after launch.

    Deliverables 04
    • →Production system · in operators' hands
    • →Runbooks & role-based training
    • →Alerting & on-call rotation
    • →Retainer · ongoing iteration loop
    Same core, long after launch. Quietly running across every brand we've shipped.
